It’s been more than a year since we parted ways. For the longest time, whenever I thought of you, all I could remember were the things that hurt me, the disappointments, the betrayals, and everything that eventually led to the end of us.

My heart was filled with so much resentment that I almost forgot how beautifully our story began.

But recently, I’ve found myself looking back at those days with a little more tenderness. And I realized that despite everything that happened between us, there are still so many things I want to thank you for.

I met you during a time when I felt like life was moving forward for everyone except me. I watched people around me chase their dreams, reach their milestones, and discover what the world had to offer, while I remained inside my little room, wondering if I would ever find the courage to step outside again.

For years, I had imprisoned myself within walls I built for my own protection. I had grown accustomed to solitude. It felt safer to remain within the confines of my little world than to risk being hurt by everything outside of it.

And then, there was you.

With your outgoing personality, your sweetness, and the ease with which you made me laugh, the world gradually felt a little less frightening. You were my friend before you became someone I loved, and I think that was one of the things I appreciated most about us.

You made companionship feel comfortable again.

Thank you for being there in 2023, when I felt so disconnected from the world and uncertain about where my life was heading. Thank you for being one of the reasons I finally found the courage to leave the prison I had built around myself.

Perhaps you never fully understood how much that meant to me.

I trusted you. I allowed myself to become vulnerable again, and for the first time in a long while, I genuinely entertained the possibility of building a future with someone.

I remember the promises I made to you. There was a time when I truly believed that after graduating from medical school, I would be ready to settle down with you. I imagined a life we could build together, and I meant every word I said when we talked about that future.

Those promises were sincere when I made them.

But somewhere along the way, things changed.

I thought you would be different. I thought that because you knew about the wounds I carried from my past, you would understand how carefully I had learned to give someone my trust.

Perhaps that was why your betrayal hurt as much as it did.

It wasn’t simply the pain of being betrayed by someone I loved. It was the realization that someone who knew how deeply I had been wounded before could still hurt me in a painfully familiar way.

There were other things, too. Things I don’t think I need to explicitly mention anymore. You know what they were, and I know how much they affected me.

For a while, what we had was beautiful. But I hope you also understand how exhausting it was to pretend that everything was fine when the very person I kept choosing to love was also the person who kept hurting me.

I hope you know how difficult it was to wake up almost every day questioning myself, wondering where I had fallen short, what I had done wrong, and whether there was something about me that made it so easy to betray my trust.

I spent so much time questioning my own worth, trying to understand why the love I was giving never seemed enough to keep certain things from happening.

And somewhere in the process of trying to preserve what we had, I began losing parts of myself again.

There was a painful irony in that. The person who once helped me find the courage to leave my little prison also became one of the reasons I felt the need to rebuild its walls.

For a long time, that was all I could think about.

But I don’t want the painful ending of our relationship to become the only thing I remember about you.

I also want to acknowledge my own shortcomings.

I’m sorry for the promises I couldn’t keep. I’m sorry for the hurtful words I said, especially during the moments when my emotions got the better of me. I’m sorry for the times I hurt you, for the tears I caused, and for the wounds I may have left behind.

I know I wasn’t a perfect partner. I had my own mistakes, my own shortcomings, and moments when I could have handled things with more patience and kindness.

The pain I experienced does not erase the pain I may have caused you. And just as I wish certain things had been done differently on your end, there are things I wish I had done differently, too.

For those things, I am genuinely sorry.

I hope someday you can forgive me, too.

It’s sad that we had to end the way we did. Almost two years of shared memories, conversations, laughter, affection, and plans for a future together, only to eventually become two people living entirely separate lives.

Sometimes, I wish our story had ended more gently. I wish we could have parted ways without leaving so much hurt in each other’s hearts.

But perhaps that was how our story was meant to end.

I am slowly learning that I can be grateful for the love I once received without dismissing the pain that came with it. I can acknowledge the beautiful things you did for me without pretending that the painful ones never happened.

Both can be true.

You were once someone who made me incredibly happy, and you were also someone who hurt me deeply.

Neither truth erases the other.

Moving forward, however, I am choosing to give more space to the happy memories we shared. I want to remember the friendship that existed before everything became complicated, the laughter, the little moments of affection, the conversations about our dreams, and the comfort of once having someone to share the ordinary parts of my life with.

I want to remember you as the person who, at one point in my life, made the world feel a little less lonely.

Thank you for accompanying me through a chapter of my life when I was still learning how to live again. Thank you for the moments when your presence made things easier. Thank you for the happiness that was real, even if it wasn’t permanent.

I don’t regret having loved you. Whatever became of us, there was a time when I loved you sincerely and genuinely believed in the future we talked about.

And I think that version of us deserves to be remembered, too.

Wherever you are in life now, I sincerely wish you well. I pray for your happiness, your good health, and the fulfillment of the dreams you once shared with me. I hope life gives you opportunities to grow, to become wiser, and to love more gently and responsibly.

I hope you find peace within yourself, just as I am learning to find mine.

There is no longer a future I wish to reclaim with you, nor a past I wish to rewrite. There is only a chapter of my life that I am finally learning to look back on without so much resentment.

Perhaps this is my way of making peace with what we once were, what we eventually became, and everything we will never be.

Thank you for being part of my life from 2023 to 2025. Thank you for the nearly two years we shared, for the good memories, and especially for being there when I was still gathering the courage to step outside my little room.

I hope that when you remember me someday, you will also remember more than just the things that went wrong. I hope you remember that there was once a girl who genuinely loved you, believed in you, and imagined a future with you.

I’m sorry that we couldn’t become the people we once promised each other we would be.

Nevertheless, I will always wish you happiness.

May we both find the peace we could not give each other.

With gratitude, apologies, and a sincere wish for your happiness,

M
