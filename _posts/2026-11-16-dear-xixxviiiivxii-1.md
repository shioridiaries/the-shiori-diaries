Ang tagal na, no? More than a decade has passed since I first met you, yet somehow, I can still vividly remember the first time I saw you on that stage sa school. I remember when you called me, the way you smiled at me, and how my heart suddenly started beating so fast. I was only fifteen back then. Hindi ko pa masyadong maintindihan kung ano ’yong nararamdaman ko, but even at that moment, I knew there was something different about you.

Thank you for talking to me. Four months lang pala ’yon, no? Ang ikli kung iisipin. Pero ang saya-saya ko noong mga panahong ’yon. Salamat sa bawat oras na ibinigay mo sa akin, sa pakikipagkulitan, at sa pakikinig sa kung anu-ano kong kwento.

I’ve always been afraid of phone calls, but somehow, you made me feel comfortable. Dumating pa sa point na inaabangan ko na rin ’yong mga tawag mo. You were the first person who made me feel nervous in the most innocent way possible.

Ikaw ’yong taong inaabangan kong dumaan sa school corridor just so I could catch a little glimpse of you. Tapos kapag nahuhuli kitang nakatingin din sa akin, hindi ko na alam kung saan ako titingin. Bigla na lang magpapanic. Hahaha. Nakakatawa kapag naaalala ko, pero ang simple lang pala ng kaligayahan ko noon.

Although we barely interacted in person, I hope you know how much I treasured everything we shared. Lahat ng messages natin, phone calls, good mornings, good nights, at kahit ’yong mga saglit na ngitian natin kapag nagkakasalubong ang mga tingin natin. You probably don’t remember most of them anymore, but I do. And I’m grateful that I still do.

Naalala ko rin ’yong mga panahong pinag-uusapan natin ’yong future natin. I remember jokingly telling you that I wanted to become a doctor someday. Sabi ko lang ’yon noon, knowing how unlikely it was for me to actually pursue that dream. Parang ang layo-layo at ang hirap abutin. Hindi ko rin naman talaga inakalang magkakaroon ako ng pagkakataon.

But strangely enough, here I am.

I don’t know if you’re aware that I actually ended up pursuing medicine. I’m already a medical clerk now. Malayo pa, alam ko, at ang dami ko pang kailangang matutunan, but I’m here. Minsan, kapag napapagod ako sa hospital, naaalala ko ’yong batang ako na minsan lang nagbiro sa’yo tungkol sa pagiging doctor. Who would’ve thought that I’d actually be living a part of that little dream someday?

I wish I could tell that fifteen-year-old girl that some of the things she once thought were impossible would eventually become part of her reality.

You know, there’s something I’ve never really told you. Thinking about our happy memories was one of the things that helped me get through some of the hardest moments of my life, especially during the pandemic.

Ang dami kong pinagdaanan noon. I went through sickness, grieved the loss of people I loved, and struggled with so many things I didn’t know how to talk about. There were days when everything felt unbearably heavy, and I didn’t know when things would get better.

Kaya sobrang na-appreciate ko ’yong mga random at napakadalang na tawag mo, pati ’yong mga simpleng pangangamusta mo. You probably had no idea how much those little gestures meant to me. Hindi mo alam kung gaano ako napapasaya ng mga ’yon, especially during the days when I was struggling. For a little while, gumagaan ’yong pakiramdam ko.

You were also one of the people I always looked forward to seeing again. Whenever life felt particularly difficult, isa ka sa mga naaalala ko.

Hindi pa ako pwedeng mamatay, magkikita pa kami ulit.

’Yan ’yong isa sa mga sinasabi ko sa sarili ko noon. Tutuparin ko pa ’yong sinabi ko sa’yo na darating din ’yong araw na kaya na kitang tingnan nang diretso sa mga mata mo, nang hindi ako nahihiya, o umiiwas ng tingin.

Ang silly pakinggan ngayon, pero that little thought gave me something to look forward to during a time when I desperately needed reasons to keep going. Thank you for that, even if you never knew.

You inspired me in so many ways. And although things didn’t turn out the way I once wished they would, I cannot deny how happy I was when you were part of my life. Thank you for making me feel loved and cared for, even if only for a little while.

Sana alam mo rin kung gaano kita kamahal noon. How genuinely I cared for you, even when I didn’t quite know how to express it.

For seven years, a little part of me waited for you.

Seven years. Medyo ilang taon din pala. Haha.

I continued living my life, met different people, pursued different dreams, and went through so many changes. Pero somewhere deep inside me, there was still a little hope that perhaps, someday, our paths would cross again at the right time.

Then, in 2023, I found out that you had already been dating someone for years.

That broke me a little. Hindi ko naman maitatanggi. There were times when I wished it had been me. There were moments when I wondered what could have happened if things had turned out differently between us.

But it’s okay.

You never asked me to wait for you, and I know you didn’t owe me the future I had quietly imagined for us. You had every right to move forward, fall in love, and find your own happiness.

And despite everything, I genuinely want you to be happy, too.

Sana alam mo rin kung gaano ako ka-proud sa’yo. When I learned that you graduated cum laude and passed your board examination, sobrang natuwa ako para sa’yo. Nakakatuwa lang isipin na ’yong taong minsan kong inaabangan sa school corridor ay unti-unti nang natutupad ’yong mga pangarap niya.

We’ve both grown so much since then. Ang dami na nating pinagdaanan nang hindi natin alam kung ano ’yong nangyayari sa buhay ng isa’t isa. We’ve become different people, pursued our own dreams, and built lives separate from each other.

But hindi mo man alam, I’ve always been silently rooting for you. And I think a part of me always will.

I hope life continues to be kind to you. Sana matupad mo pa lahat ng pangarap mo. Sana masaya ka sa kung nasaan ka ngayon. I hope you have a beautiful life, one filled with genuine love, good health, and countless reasons to be grateful.

I’ll always remember you in my prayers.

I don’t think I’ll ever completely forget you. And I don’t think I need to, either. There will always be a part of me that remembers you with so much tenderness and gratitude. Hindi dahil may hinihintay pa ako, but because you were once such a meaningful part of my life too.

Thank you for making my senior high school years memorable. Thank you for giving my younger self something to look forward to every day even when I was breaking deep inside. Thank you for the butterflies, the silly smiles, the nervousness, and the innocent happiness of liking someone for the first time.

Thank you for the memories that continued to comfort me long after we stopped being part of each other’s everyday lives.

Kung babalikan ko man ’yong mga panahong ’yon, I think I’d still be that same silly girl waiting along the school corridor, hoping to catch even just a glimpse of you.

And I wouldn’t regret it.

Hindi ko pagsisisihan na naging parte ka ng buhay ko.

Maybe someday, magkikita rin tayo ulit. And by then, I assure you, kaya na talaga kitang tingnan nang diretso sa mga mata mo. But even if that day never comes, I’m grateful that I met you at fifteen.

You may never get to read this letter. You may never know how much your brief presence in my life meant to me, how often our memories made me smile during difficult times, or how sincerely I’ve celebrated your achievements from afar.

But that’s okay. Hindi mo naman kailangang malaman lahat.

I just wanted to put these feelings into words because I think the younger version of me deserves to have her story remembered, too. She loved you sincerely, so innocently, and with all the little courage her young heart could muster.

And I’m grateful to you for giving her such beautiful memories.

Thank you for being my puppy love. Thank you for being a beautiful part of my growing-up years. And thank you, simply, for having existed in that little chapter of my life.

Wherever life takes you, I hope you’ll always find happiness.

Ingat ka palagi, ha? I’m proud of you.

With fondness and everlasting gratitude,

MJ
